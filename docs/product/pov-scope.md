# Product Scope

## In scope

- macOS capture of text, links, images, and file references.
- Keyboard-first history search, filters, selection, copy, and paste.
- Pinning, deletion, undo, import/export, Quick Look, drag/drop, and image OCR.
- Local encrypted persistence with retention, item-count, and byte-budget controls.
- Excluded applications, transient/concealed pasteboard markers, and capture pausing.
- Launch at Login, configurable panel shortcut, and Accessibility onboarding.
- Explicit iPhone clipboard capture, local history, filters, previews, and App Intents.
- Signed/notarized direct distribution, npm/Homebrew installation, and release validation.

## Out of scope

- Accounts, server-side storage, analytics, advertising, and behavioral telemetry.
- Automatic iOS background monitoring or cross-application paste.
- Cloud or peer synchronization until separately approved and threat-modeled.
- Organization tenancy, remote administration, or enterprise policy management.
- Editing arbitrary rich clipboard formats.

Out-of-scope work needs product-owner approval and an ADR when structural.
