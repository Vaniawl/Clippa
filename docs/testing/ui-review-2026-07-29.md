# UI Review Evidence — 2026-07-29

## macOS

- A 560×430-point `NSHostingView` fixture rendered the history panel at Retina scale and
  attached a PNG to the passing XCTest result.
- The panel shows the search-help control, horizontally complete filter strip, readable
  source/time metadata, selected-row actions, and unused space rather than stretched rows.
- The refreshed fixture shows the destination application, Pinned/Recent grouping,
  keyboard-command footer, and a non-blocking Undo banner without clipping at 560×430.
- Single-click selects, the exclusive double-click gesture pastes, and Return remains the
  primary keyboard paste action.
- Rows expose a default accessibility action; destructive/context actions remain
  separately labeled.
- SwiftUI previews exist for the panel and Settings.

## iPhone

- The seeded app was built, installed, and launched on iPhone 17 Pro Max / iOS 26.5.
- Runtime inspection exposed labeled Settings, filter, row, and Preview controls.
- The home screen and medium Settings sheet were visually inspected; primary content was
  readable, and lower destructive actions remain reachable by scrolling.

## Accepted limitations

- Offscreen macOS Liquid Glass rendering differs slightly from an on-screen composited
  panel, so the fixture gates dimensions, non-empty pixels, and attachment review rather
  than a brittle pixel hash.
- Real VoiceOver speech order and cross-application auto-paste require manual release
  verification on a signed build.
