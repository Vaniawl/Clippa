# Store presence and experiments

- ASO: align localized metadata/keywords and visual story with audience language and real product
  tasks; competitor positions are signals, not promises of ranking or demand. Verify current field
  lengths, territory/localization behavior and asset requirements before claiming readiness.
- Distinguish product page optimization tests from custom product pages and paid-ad variants.
  Check current eligible assets/placements; specify hypothesis, allocation/exposure, primary metric,
  guardrails and interpretation before launch. Do not assume a custom page is an A/B test.
- Coordinate actual captures/brand assets with brand-creative-designer, app/store languages with
  localization-specialist and release metadata with app-store-release-manager. Every feature,
  pricing and privacy claim needs candidate-specific evidence.
- Offer relevant organic content, PR/community or paid channel plans with owner, audience, expected
  cost and measurement limitations. A draft does not authorize outreach, spend or account changes.
- Interpret conversion with source/cohort/window and attribution coverage. Use supplied real data;
  no access means a plan and explicit unknown outcome, not a simulated campaign success.
