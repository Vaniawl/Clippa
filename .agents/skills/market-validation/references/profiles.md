# Research profiles

Choose from the brief; mixed products may need more than one profile.

| Profile | Evidence and constraints to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Consumer | Repeated user need, discovery channels, current substitutes, App Store pricing/availability, accessibility, privacy, switching effort and payment signals. Ratings and forum posts are biased signals, not representative interviews. |
| Business | User versus buyer/champion, recurring operational cost, procurement/security/data residency only where applicable, buying process, implementation/support burden, existing alternatives and observed budget evidence. |
| Developer tools | Workflow/toolchain compatibility, build-versus-buy, maintainer/team purchasing, documentation and integration cost, adoption/distribution, open-source substitutes, licensing and support. Downloads/stars do not prove revenue. |

For each profile identify product-owner capabilities from the brief. Do not substitute framework example branding or SAP/ServiceNow expertise for actual capabilities. Estimate effort with the architect when evidence is inadequate; mark unsupported timelines unknown.
