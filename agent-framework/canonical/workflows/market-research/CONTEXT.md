# Investigate one audience or market hypothesis

Purpose: investigate one audience or market hypothesis. This is the local context boundary;
[WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) remains authoritative for detailed steps and gates.

## Inputs

| Source | Scope / why |
| --- | --- |
| User request or saved task contract | Product/audience hypothesis; decision it informs; geographic/time scope; actual source access. |
| [Workflow](WORKFLOW.md) | Entry/exit criteria and only applicable stages |
| [Operating instructions](../../operating-instructions.md) | Mandatory execution/authority rules; read once per confirmed context |

## Process

Scope the hypothesis; gather dated evidence and alternatives; test contradictory signals; report uncertainty and a recommendation.

Load only the references required by the current step. Retain confirmed context;
when a dependency is unavailable, record the specific gap instead of inventing evidence.

## Outputs

Conversation findings with source ledger; requested saved report linked from the task folder; proposed backlog Candidates only.

## Human and independent gates

Evidence does not approve product scope, promotion or spend. Research remains read-only and owner approval is required to promote Candidates.
Advice stays in conversation unless saving is requested; existing role ownership applies.
