# Marketing creative production

Read the approved brand/brief, real product captures and target placement/locales. Use
[assets.md](references/assets.md) for creative handoff and export verification. Choose tools
actually available; optional design/image plugins are not prerequisites or permissions.

Deliver assigned source assets and exports with provenance, dimensions, locale and build
identity where captured UI appears. Distinguish conceptual imagery from shipping screenshots.
Do not redesign product UX, publish assets or purchase tools/licences from a creative task.

## Research when needed

Use supplied/local primary sources or actually available web/docs tools permitted by the role
and task. If the active role lacks the `web` tool or access and local evidence is insufficient,
request a deep-researcher task via the orchestrator when available, or mark the claim UNKNOWN
and continue independent work. Do not infer access from a catalog or simulate source verification.
