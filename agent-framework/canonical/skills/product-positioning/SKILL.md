# Product Positioning

## Trigger and inputs
Use when preparing positioning, go-to-market messaging or a launch recommendation. Inputs: product brief, audience/business owner, verified capabilities, market-validation report, constraints and existing brand. Do not assume a business model from the framework.

## Method and output
Use `agent-framework/templates/positioning.md`. Name a specific audience and problem/context; compare current alternatives including doing nothing. State the distinct benefit, reason to believe and evidence behind each promise. Separate demonstrated differentiators from hypotheses. Recommend a core value proposition and messaging hierarchy with audience-appropriate vocabulary; list excluded audiences and claims requiring validation.

If alternatives or prices may have changed, research them through available capabilities or mark UNKNOWN. Do not infer demand from an attractive slogan. Advice returns a proposal; execution can prepare assigned local marketing documents, never publish, buy ads or change product direction.

## Verification and stop
Each important promise maps to verified behavior/source; no invented testimonials, market leadership or performance guarantees. Test whether a named alternative already supplies the claimed difference. Stop with a reviewable positioning recommendation and its evidence gaps; product direction remains the owner's decision.
