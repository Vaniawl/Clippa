# Market Validation

## Trigger and inputs
Use when answering a market question that informs a product decision. Inputs: product brief, audience/business owner, geography, relevant date, question and research budget. Follow the repository research policy. If web access is missing, report that limit and use supplied sources; do not simulate a current search.

## Method and output
Select the appropriate [research profile](references/profiles.md). Compare named direct alternatives, indirect/manual substitutes and doing nothing. Prefer primary product/pricing documentation; distinguish an advertised capability from observed customer outcomes. Track source URL, publication/update and access date, applicability and contradictions. Published pricing supports what is offered, not proof of actual willingness to pay.

Return a neutral report covering question, audience, problem evidence, alternatives, demand/payment signals, feasibility constraints, claims labeled Fact / Inference / Uncertain, source ledger, gaps and a recommended probe. Scores are optional decision aids; unknown dimensions remain unscored. AI personas provide simulated hypotheses, never customer interviews or observed demand.

## Verification and stop
Every decision-driving market claim has a dated applicable source or UNKNOWN. Include evidence against the favored idea. Do not carry over a consultancy, enterprise buyer or vendor stack unless the product brief says so. Advice does not edit tracked files or approve priorities. Stop when the assigned question is answered within budget or remaining gaps are explicitly unknown.
