# App Store Marketing

## Trigger and inputs
Use when preparing App Store presence or launch preparation. Inputs: approved positioning, actual feature availability, platform/locales, audience, verified product screenshots, constraints and current store rules. Verify current requirements from official Apple sources when tools permit; otherwise mark rule-dependent checks NOT RUN and do not claim upload readiness.

## Method and output
Use `agent-framework/templates/launch-plan.md`. Prepare local title/subtitle/description/keyword drafts where applicable, screenshot storyboard anchored in real user tasks, accessibility/localization notes, support/privacy destinations, launch checklist and proposed channels. Make each screenshot claim consistent with the shipping build; distinguish conceptual art from captured UI. Check pricing, availability, permissions and data promises against the product. Do not add tracking, subscriptions or a backend automatically.

Propose a bounded page experiment with hypothesis, audience, primary metric, measurement method, guardrails, exposure/window and interpretation rules using product-measurement. Publishing, App Store Connect changes, outreach and ad spend are separate external actions.

## Verification and stop
Link metadata promises to actual behavior/evidence; validate applicable lengths and assets against dated official requirements. Record build/revision/device/locale for captured screenshots. Label unavailable captures/manual checks NOT RUN. Stop when assigned local materials and readiness gaps are delivered; never claim launched or validated without real delivery/outcome data.
