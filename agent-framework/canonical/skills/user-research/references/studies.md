# Study plans and synthesis

- Choose the decision first: comprehension, task success, friction or unmet need. Use neutral
  tasks/questions and realistic success/failure definitions; avoid leading participants toward
  the desired solution. Specify target audience, recruitment proposal and study limitations.
- For actual observations retain sanitized IDs, date, product/build, task context and provenance.
  Distinguish quotes, observed behavior, interpretation and recommended action. Never manufacture
  quotations or treat persona outputs as participants.
- Report recurring findings with denominators where meaningful; a small qualitative sample does
  not provide population conversion rates. Include disconfirming evidence, sampling bias and
  alternative explanations; avoid ranking demand from a convenience sample alone.
- For TestFlight feedback separate installation/runtime defects from user-value judgments. Route
  reproduction evidence to QA/builders, support needs to support and priorities to PM/owner.
- Keep consent/use/retention constraints explicit. De-identify exports and do not commit raw personal
  recordings/transcripts. If no actual data exists, return a study plan with required inputs.
