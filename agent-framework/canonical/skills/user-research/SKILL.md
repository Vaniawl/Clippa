# User research

Identify the product decision, audience, actual available observations and consent/use
constraints. Use [studies.md](references/studies.md) for study planning or synthesis.
Deliver a decision-relevant plan or evidence-linked findings. No recruitment, contact or
interviews are authorized by this method; simulation cannot establish demand or usability.

Use anonymized observation IDs and preserve uncertainty. Missing participants/data means
a plan or unknown result. The user-researcher role is read-only; authorized saving can be
performed by the coordinator without expanding the researcher's authority.

## Research when needed

Use supplied/local primary sources or actually available web/docs tools permitted by the role
and task. If the active role lacks the `web` tool or access and local evidence is insufficient,
request a deep-researcher task via the orchestrator when available, or mark the claim UNKNOWN
and continue independent work. Do not infer access from a catalog or simulate source verification.
